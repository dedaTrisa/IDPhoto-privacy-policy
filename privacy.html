ID Photo Maker - 隐私政策 (中文)

应用信息

应用名称: ID Photo Maker（证件照制作）

开发者: Vladimir Gavranic

适用版本: 1.0.0 及以上

数据收集与使用

本应用对您通过系统图片选择器自行选择的一张照片进行裁剪，并生成各种尺寸的证件照和打印版面。照片仅在您的设备上处理，本应用不会上传、发送或共享。本应用不联网，没有账户，不使用统计分析或广告，不使用相机，也不识别人脸。我们不会收集、传输或与任何人共享您的数据。

本应用不保留您照片的副本，退出应用后所选照片即被遗忘。应用仅在设备上保存上次使用的设置（尺寸、纸张、背景等）。本应用已关闭云备份。

权限说明

本应用不申请任何权限，包括网络权限、存储权限和相机权限。照片通过 Android 系统图片选择器打开；您保存的图片会写入手机的 Pictures/IDPhoto 文件夹。

分享

如果您点击“Share”（分享），图片将交给您自己选择的应用（例如电子邮件或即时通讯），去向完全由您决定，开发者不会收到任何内容。

数据主体权利

已保存的图片位于您的相册中，您可以随时自行删除。卸载应用将删除应用设置；已保存的图片会保留，直到您自行删除。

免责声明

各国、各类证件对官方照片的要求（头部大小、背景颜色、表情、眼镜等）各不相同，本应用不会检查这些要求，头部参考线仅供参考。请务必查看接收照片的机构或网站的具体要求。本应用不保证照片一定会被接受。

联系方式

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 gavranic76@gmail.com 联系我们。

ID Photo Maker - Privacy Policy (English)

App Information

App name: ID Photo Maker

Developer: Vladimir Gavranic

Applies to: version 1.0.0 and later

Data Collection and Usage

The app crops one picture that you choose yourself with the Android system picture picker and makes ID photos and print sheets from it. The picture is processed only on your device; the app does not upload, send or share it. The app does not use the internet and has no accounts, analytics or advertising. It does not use the camera and does not recognise faces. We do not collect, transmit or share your data with anyone.

The app does not keep a copy of your photo: after you leave the app the chosen picture is forgotten. It only keeps its last settings (size, paper, background) on the device. Cloud backup is switched off for this app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions, including no network, storage or camera permission. The picture is opened through the Android system picker, and the pictures you save are written into your phone's Pictures/IDPhoto folder.

Sharing

If you tap "Share", the picture is handed to the app you choose (for example e-mail or a messenger). Where it goes is entirely your decision; the developer receives nothing.

Your Rights

The saved pictures are in your gallery and you can delete them yourself at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its settings; the saved pictures stay until you delete them.

Disclaimer

The rules for official photos (head size, background colour, expression, glasses and so on) differ by country and document. The app does not check them and the head guide is only a help. Always read the requirements of the office or website that will receive the photo. The app does not promise that a photo will be accepted.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at gavranic76@gmail.com.
